The Stats

New to basketball? That's okay!

Here you can learn what the statistics we show mean.


Games Played
 The number of games played throughout the time period.

(GP) - Games Played

Starting a Game
 Starters are players in the team's initial lineup and typically play the most minutes for the team. These positions are typically given to the best players on the team. Starters can fluctuate game to game based on injuries, rest, or other external factors.

(GS) - Games Started

Minutes Played
 Number of played in a basketball game.

(MIN) - Minutes Played

Field Goals
 Number of baskets a player made or attempted for 2 or 3 points.

(FG-M:A) - Field Goals | Made:Attempt Ratio
(FG%) - Field Goals | Scoring Percentage
(FG3-M:A) - Field Goals (3 Pointers) | Made:Attempt Ratio
(FG3%) - Field Goals (3 Pointers) | Scoring Percentage

Free Throws
 Uncontested shots made or attempted for one point after a foul under specific circumstances.

(FT-M:A) - Free Throws | Made:Attempt Ratio
(FT%) - Free Throws | Scoring Percentage

Personal Fouls
 Illegal physical contact or a rule violation that interferes with play, such as pushing, improper blocking, and more. Players and teams can only commit so many fouls in a game. Any number exceeding that increases the types of fouls that can cause free throws. Examples of types of fouls are listed below:

Shooting Foul - Illegal contact made during a shot attempt
Flagrant Foul - Excessive or dangerous contact
Technical Foul - Unsportsmanlike conduct or rule violations

(PF) - Personal Fouls
(PFPG) - Personal Fouls Per Game

Turnovers
 When a player loses possession of the ball to the other team. Having the ball stolen, a pass received by the other team, and stepping out of bounds with the ball are all examples of a turnover.

(TOV) - Turnovers
(TPG) - Turnovers Per Game

Blocks
 Occurs when a player on defense deflects an opponent's shot going upward before reaching the basket. Once a shot ball is on its downward path, any interference is a goaltending foul.

(BLK) - Blocks
(BPG) - Blocks Per Game

Steals
 When a defensive player legally takes the ball from an opponent when it is in their possession.

(STL) - Steals
(SPG) - Steals Per Game

Assists
 Happens when a player makes a pass that directly leads to a basket made.

(AST) - Assists
(APG) - Assists Per Game

Rebounds
 Occurs when a player retrieves the ball after a shot on the basket has been missed.

Offensive Rebound - Missed shot retrieved by the team that shot the ball
Defensive Rebound - Missed shot retrieved by the team that was defending

(REB) - Rebounds
(REB-O:D) - Rebounds | Offensive:Defensive Ratio
(RPG) - Rebounds Per Game

Points
 Points scored after making a basket.

(PTS) - Points
(PPG) - Points Per Game


NOTE: The stats provided on this website are complied using NBA data. Some data from the early days of the NBA may be missing which can result in inaccurate data when getting stats from that era.
